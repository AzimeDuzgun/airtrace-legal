AirTrace Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AirTrace handles information when you use the AirTrace mobile application.

Information We Collect

AirTrace may collect limited technical and usage information necessary to operate, maintain, analyze, and improve the application.

This may include:

  • Device and operating system information
  • Application usage and interaction information
  • Diagnostic and crash information
  • Technical identifiers and related analytics data

Bluetooth

AirTrace uses Bluetooth functionality to discover compatible nearby devices and estimate proximity using Bluetooth signal strength.

Bluetooth access is used to provide the application's device discovery and proximity features.

Location Information

AirTrace does not use location information to track your physical movements or create a history of your location.

Depending on the device and operating system version, certain system permissions may be associated with Bluetooth functionality. AirTrace uses these permissions only as necessary to provide device discovery features.

Firebase Services

AirTrace uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Remote Config.

These services may process technical, diagnostic, and usage information to help understand application performance, diagnose crashes, configure application behavior, and improve the user experience.

Third-Party Services

Information processed through third-party services is subject to the privacy practices of those service providers.

AirTrace currently uses services provided by Google Firebase.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through AirTrace. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

AirTrace is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AirTrace's features, services, or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
